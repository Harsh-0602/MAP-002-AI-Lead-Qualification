# MAP-002 – AI Lead Qualification System

## Overview

This AI workflow automatically qualifies incoming leads using Google Gemini and stores the result inside Google Sheets.

---

## Business Problem

Sales teams waste time manually reviewing every lead before deciding who deserves immediate attention.

---

## Solution

This workflow:

- Receives a lead
- Uses AI to analyze buying intent
- Assigns a Lead Score
- Classifies the lead
- Stores everything automatically
